How To Measure

Getting The Perfect Fit Is Easy

Use a soft measuring tape and follow these simple steps to ensure the best fit for your child. For the most accurate measurements, keep the tape comfortably snug without pulling too tightly.

01

Chest

Measure around the fullest part of the chest while keeping the tape level.

02

Waist

Measure around the natural waistline without tightening the tape.

03

Height

Measure from the top of the head to the bottom of the feet while standing straight.

04

Hip

Measure around the fullest part of the hips for a comfortable fit.

Fit Tips & Care Guide

Helpful Tips For The Best Fit & Long-Lasting Comfort

Follow these simple recommendations to choose the right size and keep your Little Loom outfits looking beautiful for every adventure.

Fit Tips

  • Measure your child while standing naturally.
  • If between two sizes, choose the larger size.
  • Allow room for comfortable move and growth.
  • Compare measurements with our size chart.

Care Guide

  • Machine wash using cold or lukewarm water.
  • Use a mild detergent to protect fabric quality.
  • Avoid bleach and harsh chemicals.
  • Dry naturally and iron on low heat if needed.
